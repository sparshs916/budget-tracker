Accounts

Unchecked accounts are left out of “Budget accounts”. To track savings (an account you put money away in), set its type to Savings and untick it: money you move into it shows as Savings, money you move back out reduces Savings, and its interest stays with it. Ticked, moves between it and your other accounts just cancel out. SimpleFIN doesn’t say what type each account is, so the type is a guess from its name. Fix it here if it’s wrong: it decides how card payments and transfers are counted.

For accounts SimpleFIN can't connect (like Apple Card). Fill them with CSV uploads.

Connect your banks

Add an account by hand

Upload transactions from a CSV

Download the CSV from your bank's website. The file doesn't say which account it's from, so pick that here (or add a new account). Rows already in the app are skipped, so overlapping or repeated uploads are safe. You'll see a preview before anything is saved.
